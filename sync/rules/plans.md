# Architecture and plans

- Architecture follows the architect skill: users and how they reach the service (including a non-digital route), components, how it works, duties by design, risks, and a Mermaid diagram.
- Plans follow the plan skill's template: user need, the real words users will read, acceptance criteria someone can check, files, tests, compliance documents, risks.
- Name teams and roles, never residents. No personal data.
