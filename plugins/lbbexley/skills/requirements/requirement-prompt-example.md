# Requirement prompt: an example

An illustration of the level of detail that helps, for an invented staff tool. It isn't a project to build: describe your own in [requirement-prompt.md](requirement-prompt.md).

## What we want to build

A way for staff to book meeting rooms at the Civic Offices and see which are free.

## Why

Bookings are in a shared spreadsheet. Rooms are double-booked about twice a week, and nobody can check bookings on a phone.

## Who uses it

Any member of staff books rooms. The facilities team manages the rooms and can cancel bookings. Staff without a laptop ask the facilities team to book for them.

## How it works today

Staff add a row to the spreadsheet. The facilities team checks it each morning and emails anyone who's double-booked.

## What it must do

- Show which rooms are free at a given time
- Book a room for up to a day, and cancel your own booking
- Let the facilities team close a room for maintenance

## What's out of scope

- Booking for people outside the council
- Catering

## Rules, policies and deadlines

Bookings longer than a day need the facilities manager's approval (facilities policy, section 3).

## Information about people

The name and team of the person booking, so others know who to ask about a booking. Kept for 3 months.

## Systems

None at first. Outlook calendars, perhaps, later.
