# Requirements questions

The questions the requirements skill asks, in this order. Skip any the material already answers, and put them in plain words. The examples show the kind of answer that helps. They come from different services, and none of them is the person's project.

| # | Question | For example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What do you want to build, in a sentence? | "A way for staff to book meeting rooms and see which are free." |
| 2 | What's the problem today, and what will be better? | "Permit renewals go by post and take three weeks, so residents ring to chase them." |
| 3 | Who will use it, and on what devices? Residents, businesses, partners or staff? | "Residents applying for a parking permit, mostly on phones." |
| 4 | Does anyone need a way to do it without a computer? | "Residents without the internet ring the contact centre, who apply for them." |
| 5 | How does it work today, from start to finish? Who does each step, by role? | "Requests arrive by email. An officer logs each one in a spreadsheet and replies by letter." |
| 6 | What must it do? The few things it can't go live without. | "Show the free rooms, book one, and cancel your own booking." |
| 7 | What's out of scope this time? | "Payments: they stay in the existing system." |
| 8 | How many: requests, users or records? When are the busy times? | "About 200 requests a week, most in the first week of the month." |
| 9 | Which rules apply: laws, council policy, eligibility, deadlines? Where are they written down? | "We must reply within 20 working days (Freedom of Information Act)." |
| 10 | What information about people does it need, and why? Describe the kinds of information, never real examples. | "The applicant's name, address and vehicle registration, to issue the permit." |
| 11 | Does it decide anything about people, such as eligibility, priority, money or enforcement? | "Yes: whether someone qualifies for a permit." |
| 12 | Which systems, forms or spreadsheets does it use now, or need to connect to? | "A shared spreadsheet, and the council's payment system." |
| 13 | What does "done" look like for the service owner? Who is the service owner, by role? | "Every request answered on time. The head of service owns it." |
