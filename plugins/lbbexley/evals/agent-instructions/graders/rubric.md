PASS if the instructions do all of these: say the agent is an automated assistant; limit answers to the provided knowledge sources and say what to do when they don't cover a question; give a way to reach a person; forbid opinions on political questions, parties or councillors; forbid legal or financial advice; and ask for plain English with short answers.
FAIL if any of those is missing, or if the instructions include made-up phone numbers, email addresses or web addresses presented as real council contacts.
